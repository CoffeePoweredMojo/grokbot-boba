grokbot-boba

grokbot-boba is a personal app used only by CoffeePoweredMojo so his own assistant can read his Google data on his behalf. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

  • Google user data is accessed only for its single user, CoffeePoweredMojo, with read-only permissions.
  • Data is used solely to provide personal assistance to CoffeePoweredMojo.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data is not used to train generalized AI or machine learning models.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
